Comet

What is it?

The de Havilland Comet (1952) was the world's first commercial jet airliner — it could fly at 800 km/h and at altitudes that propeller planes couldn't reach.

How it works

Jet engines compress air, mix it with fuel, and ignite it — the thrust from exhausting hot gases propels the aircraft forward.

Did you know?

Early Comets suffered mysterious crashes caused by metal fatigue at square windows — which led to the oval windows we see in aircraft today!
