Coal Fields

What is it?

Coal fields are regions where coal deposits occur in thick underground seams — India's largest are in the Damodar Valley (Jharkhand/West Bengal).

How it works

Raniganj (1774) was India's first coal mine; the Jharia field still holds one of the world's largest coking coal reserves.

Did you know?

Some coal seams in Jharia have been burning underground for over 100 years — an underground fire that no one has been able to extinguish!
