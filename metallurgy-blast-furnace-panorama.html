Blast Furnace — Panorama

What is it?

This panoramic model shows the complete blast furnace complex — from ore storage and skip cars to the tapping floor where molten iron flows out.

How it works

Hot blast air (up to 1,200°C) is blasted in through nozzles called tuyeres; the entire furnace runs continuously for months without shutting down.

Did you know?

A large modern blast furnace taps molten iron every 45 minutes — that's enough metal to fill a swimming pool each day!
