Mineral Map of India

What is it?

India is rich in minerals — coal in Jharkhand and Odisha; iron ore in Goa and Chhattisgarh; bauxite in Odisha; copper in Rajasthan; and more.

How it works

This map shows where different minerals are found across India, helping understand why certain industries are located in specific regions.

Did you know?

India has the world's 4th largest coal reserves — enough to last for over 100 years at current usage rates!
