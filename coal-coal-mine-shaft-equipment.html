Coal Mine Shaft & Equipment

What is it?

A coal mine shaft is a vertical tunnel sunk into the earth to access underground coal seams — equipped with lifts (cages), ventilation, and water pumps.

How it works

Miners descend the shaft in cages; cutting machines, roof supports, and conveyor belts work together to extract and transport coal safely.

Did you know?

The deepest coal mine shafts in the world go over 3,000 metres underground — where temperatures can reach 60°C even without any fire!
